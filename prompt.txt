Write one fortune for a houseplant sitting in a pot on a windowsill.

The fortune must be a single line, 1 to 120 characters long. It should
sound like a real fortune — a gentle prediction about the plant's own
future — and it should be kind and funny. Make it feel written for this
one plant in this one window, not for plants in general: mention the
window, the glass, the sun, or the windowsill itself. No care tips, no
slogans, no captions. Just the fortune, plain text, nothing else.
