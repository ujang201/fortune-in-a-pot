A single kind, funny fortune for the houseplant in the pot on the windowsill — one line, 120 characters or fewer, written as if the plant itself were having its future told.

## The entry

> By spring your tallest leaf will touch the glass, and the whole windowsill will lean in to hear your news.

## Why this fortune

A fortune should sound like a prediction, not advice. This one gives the plant a small triumph of its own — the tallest leaf reaching the glass — and lets the rest of the windowsill gather round to hear about it. Kind, a little funny, and written for this one plant in this one window.

## How to run

```sh
node main.ts
```

This writes the fortune to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when `OUTPUT_DIR` is set). The program checks the line is a single line of 1–120 characters before writing it, then reads the file back to confirm it matches exactly.
